#Productivity 

1) Write the magic number 1440 and tape it in strategic places
	1) Taping this number in strategic places will remind you of the value of your time and how to invest wisely. You can tape it anywhere to your place of vision
2) Start doing the **MIT** (Most important task)
	1) Write your goal down
		1) Daily goal
		2) Weekly goal
		3) Monthly goal
		4) Period
	2) Identify all the tasks/activities that are necessary to achieve your goal
		1) Write all the important tasks well before executing
	3) Circle the task that will have the biggest impact on your goal
	4) Schedule your MIT for the first two hours after your fully awake
		1) Try to schedule these MIT's in the first two hours to improve the cognitive workload
3) Work from a calendar, Not a to-do-list
	1) Schedule a chunk of time for everything important for you #google_calendar #notion_calendar
	2) Schedule important tasks earlier in the day
		1) Plan #MIT early in the first hours of the day
		2) Unforeseen situations are certain to come up and steal your time later in the day
		3) Reschedule goals instead of cancelling them
		4) Schedule time for breaks too
			1) Use this time to relax and reorganize your thoughts
4) Become your ideal future self today
	1) Commit to being the person you want to become in the future
		1) We all have some vision of ourselves in the future - an image of the person we want to become. To become that person, we must start at some point in time. So why don't you decide to become that person now?
		2) Dream big
			1) Procrastination is not about laziness. It happens because our dreams are not big enough.
		3) Get someone to be accountable to
			1) It is easier to break a promise to yourself than to others.
		4) Settle for good enough
			1) We procrastinate because finishing something is too difficult Once you agree that imperfection is okay, you will feel more motivated to finish the task.
5) Always carry a notebook with you
	1) Jot down everything and anything you don't wanna forget.
		1) Record random creative ideas you have, like a new book you want to write, a new company you want to start, or a product you want to invest.
